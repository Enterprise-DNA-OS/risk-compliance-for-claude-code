# Camms target research

Checked 9 October 2026. Family: enterprise risk and compliance (GRC), new to the rebuild registry. Distinct from the health and safety build, which covers HSWA incidents and site hazards rather than the enterprise register, controls, obligations and operational resilience.

## The bill

Camms has no public price. [cammsgroup.com](https://cammsgroup.com/) now reads "Camms Is Now Part of Riskonnect" and offers only "Request a demo". Public procurement records give a sense of scale, not a price list:

- UK Debt Management Office, 2019: about GBP 16,800 for one year of risk register, incident and action tracking expected to be Cammsrisk ([Contracts Finder](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/)).
- Brighton and Hove City Council: GBP 72,000 for March 2020 to February 2022, a hosted performance and risk system holding the council's strategic and directorate risks.
- Sutherland Shire Council (NSW): AUD 363,440 including GST over five years from April 2020 for Camms program management software (a different module).

These are old public contract values, not current quotes and not NZ or AU risk-module invoices.

## Who pays for this kind of system

- Councils, which hold strategic and operational risk registers for audit and risk committees.
- APRA-regulated entities (super funds, insurers, banks, credit unions and mutuals), which have had to meet CPS 230 since 1 July 2025: critical operations, tolerance levels, annual continuity exercises, a material service provider register, and 72-hour and 24-hour notices.
- Not-for-profits, advice firms and professional practices with a board risk committee.
